Privacy Policy — Party Words

Developer: Emir Doğrucu (com.emirdogrucu.partywords)

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Party Words is a free party game that may show ads and offer optional in-app purchases, including consumable credits for AI-generated word decks.

Data we collect

  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Purchase data: App Store / Play Store process payments; we do not store payment card details. Purchase tokens may be sent to our deck API to grant credits.
  • Local preferences: Language, sound, purchase status, credit cache, and custom decks stay on your device.
  • AI deck generation: Your prompt and an anonymous device identifier are sent to our API (and may go to a language-model provider such as OpenAI) to create cards and prevent abuse. Do not put personal data in prompts.
  • Crash logs: If Sentry is enabled in a release build, stack traces may be sent. Prompts, receipts, and device ids are stripped.

Data we do not collect

We do not operate user accounts or collect names, emails, or location for core gameplay.

Children

The core game is intended for ages 12+. Optional adult packs require in-app age confirmation and are hidden by default.

Your rights

Uninstalling the app deletes local data. For GDPR requests contact the developer.
